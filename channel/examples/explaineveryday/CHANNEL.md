# EXAMPLE: the first channel's profile, filled in (ExplainEveryday). Copy ideas, not values.

## 1. Identity
- Channel name: ExplainEveryday (handle @ExplainEverydayHQ)
- Tagline: Big Questions. Simple Explanations.
- Niche: everyday money, science and how-things-work explainers (first film: how banks earn from a credit card; others: dark matter / Euclid, Greek myth, AI voice scams)
- Audience: curious viewers who want plain explanations; first films in Hindi for India, with English subtitles
- Film length: 6-10 minutes
- Cadence: one carefully researched film per week was the plan

## 2. Language plan
- Narration: Hindi (voice text in Devanagari; a Roman Hinglish line per chunk for the caption record)
- On-screen text: English, always
- Subtitles: English on top, Devanagari below (`subtitles_en-hindi.srt`); also separate English and Hindi `.srt` files for upload

## 3. Host and look
- Host: ExplainEveryDayHost, a boy (curly dark hair, navy hooded jacket, red scarf, white tee, olive cargo trousers, white sneakers); male narration voice
- Styles used so far (topic -> style): credit cards -> sketchnote and midcentury-toon; dark matter / Euclid -> flat-space-explainer (library style); antibiotic resistance + AI -> midcentury-toon (built before the per-topic rule, the library was not surveyed). Since 2026-11-24 the style is chosen by each topic.
- Thumbnails: big white words with ink plate, the host surprised, one number badge, the hero object in a clear zone, mustard-yellow ground

## 4. Voice and sound
- Voice: `hi-deva` (hi-IN-MadhurNeural) for synthetic; the owner also records their own voice for some films (aligned with the ASR tools)
- Music: NONE in any film (the owner removed it everywhere); keep sound effects and a quiet room tone
- Loudness: -14 LUFS

## 5. Rules for every film
- No "LemoLab"/library sign-off in the film; credits in `CREDITS`
- Every number on screen from the approved script; illustrative numbers labelled (e.g. the 1.5 % merchant fee is an example)
- Finance topics: general education, not advice, "read your card's terms"
- Confirm before uploads or anything outward-facing

## 6. YouTube settings
- Language Hindi, category Education, not made for kids; subtitles uploaded; custom thumbnail needs a verified channel
- The synthetic-voice question is answered by the owner per film
