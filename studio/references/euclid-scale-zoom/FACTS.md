# Facts used in the film, and how well each is supported

Checked on 20 Nov 2026 through several news reports of ESA's 12 Nov 2026 "DR1-Foundation" Euclid release (physicsworld.com, livescience.com, outlookmoney-style summaries, newspaceeconomy.ca, iflscience.com). ESA's own announcement page was not opened directly: confirm the figures on https://www.esa.int before publishing.

| Claim in the film | Wording on screen / in the voice | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Euclid has catalogued 900 million+ galaxies | "सूचीबद्ध" (catalogued), counter 900M+ | Several reports of the release. "Catalogued", not "discovered". |
| About 15,000 lens candidates | "≈ 15,000", "LENS CANDIDATES", "all still need confirming" | Reports describe candidates; some sources call them high-confidence. The film says candidates. |
| Early data, not the finished map; full first release due mid-2027 | "अभी अधूरा नक्शा", "दो हज़ार सत्ताईस के मध्य तक" | Reports say weak-lensing and clustering products arrive mid-2027, completing Data Release 1. |
| Dark matter is inferred from gravity and has never been photographed | "INFERRED · NOT PHOTOGRAPHED", narration | General scientific consensus; the halo and web are drawn as dashed measurements. |
| Gravitational lensing and Einstein rings | narration, inset | Textbook physics; the drawings are schematic. |
| Dark energy is a separate mystery tied to expansion | narration, left half of the split scene | General scientific consensus. |
| Euclid sits about 1.5 million km from Earth (L2) | "15 लाख किमी", ribbon "1.5 million km" | Standard figure for the Sun-Earth L2 point; not re-checked against ESA's page. |
| India's GMRT is a radio telescope, a different instrument from Euclid | narration, "GMRT · INDIA · RADIO WAVES" | Operated by NCRA-TIFR near Pune; Euclid does not use GMRT. Ribbon figure "25 km" is the commonly quoted spread of the dishes: check on https://www.gmrt.ncra.tifr.res.in. |
| Galaxy size about 100,000 light-years; cluster halo about 10 million light-years | ribbon "field of view" readout | Round, illustrative figures for scale; they are not measurements of a specific object. |

Not claimed anywhere: "1,900 square degrees" (only one source gave it) and the "60 million quasar candidates" figure.
Dark matter and dark energy are never used as the same thing.
