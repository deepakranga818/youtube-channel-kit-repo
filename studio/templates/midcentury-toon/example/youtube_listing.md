📌 IN THIS VIDEO
• The four players behind every swipe: issuer (your bank), merchant, acquirer / payment service provider, and the card network (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
• Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) with a simple example: ₹10,000 purchase, 1.5% fee = ₹150, merchant receives ₹9,850
• Who shares that ₹150: acquirer / PSP, card network and the interchange fee that goes to the issuing bank
• Interest and finance charges when you pay only the minimum due
• Joining / annual fees, cash advance fee, forex markup and the MITC (Most Important Terms and Conditions)
• Why cashback and rewards are not free money, and why a bank's profit is never guaranteed

⚠️ NOTE
The 1.5% fee is a hypothetical example to explain the idea, not a standard MDR rate. Real fees vary by card type, merchant category, payment setup and agreement. This video is general education, not financial advice. Please read your own card's terms and charges (MITC) before you decide.
