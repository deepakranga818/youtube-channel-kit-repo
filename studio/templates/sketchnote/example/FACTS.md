# Facts in "How Banks Earn From a Credit Card"

All claims come from the approved script (`../credit-card-video/script.md`). No external source was consulted; the film is a general explainer, not financial advice.

| Claim (screen / voice) | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A card payment involves four parties: issuer (your bank), merchant, acquirer / payment service provider, card network (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) | Standard description of the card-payment ecosystem | Simplified; real flows have more intermediaries |
| The merchant pays a Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) fee to accept card payments | Standard | |
| Worked example: ₹10,000 purchase, 1.5 % fee = ₹150, merchant receives ₹9,850 | Arithmetic: 10,000 × 0.015 = 150; 10,000 − 150 = 9,850 | **Labelled "hypothetical" and "illustrative, not a standard MDR rate" on screen and in the voice**; real fees vary by card type, merchant category and setup |
| The ₹150 is shared among acquirer / PSP, card network and interchange (paid to the issuing bank) | Standard description of fee components | **The film never says the issuer gets all of it**; the pie is marked "split varies, not to scale" |
| Bar of ₹9,850 vs ₹150 is drawn to scale (1.5 %); the pie of the ₹150 is not | Drawn from the numbers | |
| Paying the full statement amount by the due date means no interest on eligible purchases (interest-free period) | Standard card terms | Conditions differ by issuer; the voice says "eligible purchases" |
| Paying only the minimum due leaves a balance that attracts interest | Standard card terms | |
| Other income: joining / annual fees, cash-advance fee, forex markup, some service charges | Standard card terms | "Not every card has all of these" is stated |
| Cash withdrawal at an ATM with a credit card usually carries a cash-advance fee plus interest | Standard card terms | Voice: "can apply" |
| Rewards are funded partly by merchant-side income and partnerships; they are not free money | Script's framing | Annual fee, redemption limits and minimum spend are named as conditions |
| Bank profit is not guaranteed: costs include rewards, fraud prevention, processing, support, technology, acquisition, unpaid dues | Script's framing | |
